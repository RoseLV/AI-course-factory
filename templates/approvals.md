# Approvals: {slug}

Append-only. One line per human decision. Written only by the main session, never by a subagent.
Each line quotes the human's own words verbatim from the reply to the gate request.

Format: `date | gate | decision | "human's words"`

Decision is `APPROVED` or `REJECTED`. A rejection line is followed by the notes sent to the
owning agent.
